# Venue quality and metric provenance

Conference papers are not assigned a Journal Impact Factor. ICRA/IROS are prioritized robotics conferences; RTCSA and EMSOFT are directly relevant embedded-systems venues. The F1TENTH PMLR item is explicitly a NeurIPS Competition & Demonstration Track publication, not a main-track NeurIPS paper.

The following **historical, explicitly dated** values are used as evidence of venue priority, not current-year claims or paper-quality scores. A journal's quartile can vary across categories, metric systems and years.

| Venue | Metric year / source | IF | Quartile | Category limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters | 2023 JCR, released June 2024; IEEE January 2025 title list | 4.6 | Q2 as reported | Summary does not give the category; no category-specific claim made |
| IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics | Same source | 6.1 | Q1 as reported | Same limitation |

Source: [IEEE title list, January 2025](https://open.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/IEEE-Title-List-January-2025.pdf), RA-L on page 2, TMECH on page 4; metric-year footnote on page 4. These are deliberately not labeled “2026 Q1/Q2”. The [RA-L society page](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ieee-robotics-and-automation-letters/) may display newer IF values; changing an IF does not independently verify a quartile.

For the remaining journals, this release does **not** assert a verified year/category-specific JCR quartile. This includes Journal of Field Robotics, Communications Engineering, Sensors, Robotics, Frontiers in Neurorobotics, IJECE and JCCT. They were screened for topic relevance and primary-source evidence; their inclusion should not be read as a Q1/Q2 certification. The publisher name alone is insufficient. Scopus CiteScore/SJR Q1 is not interchangeable with JCR Q1.

The list therefore prioritizes reputable venues and directly relevant work while preserving evidence gaps. Add a new rank only with the metric system, metric year, category and a verifiable source.

## Additional venues in the expanded release

Nature Communications, Scientific Reports and IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems were added based on publication identity and topic relevance. No new year/category-specific quartile is asserted without matching evidence. TinyLidarNet is an IROS 2024 paper. NeoRacer and alpha-RPO remain explicitly labeled preprints.

## CVF and IEEE expansion

CVF records are classified by the actual venue: ICCV main conference, CVPR/ICCV Workshops, or ACCV. IEEE is a publisher, not a uniform venue-quality tier. ICRA 2022 and ICAR 2023 are distinct venues. arXiv-only records remain preprints. No additional JCR quartile or conference Impact Factor is inferred from these sources.
